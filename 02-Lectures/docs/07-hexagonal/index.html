Hexagonal Architecture

Ports & Adapters for clean, testable applications — Duration: 2h · Instructor: Olivier Planson

Hexagonal Ports Adapters Clean Arch DIP Testing

Learning Objectives

✅Understand the principles of hexagonal architecture and its benefits
✅Apply the Ports and Adapters pattern
✅Implement the Dependency Inversion Principle (DIP)
✅Design a clean architecture with layer separation

  6. Implementing ports and adapters
  7. Testing strategies with hexagonal
  8. Refactoring from DDD to hexagonal
